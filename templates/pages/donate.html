Donor FAQs

Clear answers before you give.

What causes can I support?

Seven causes in all: Urban Forestry and Climate Action, Skill Development and Employment Generation, the Generics for All Initiative, Plastic Waste Management, Farmer Producer Organizations, Education and Financial Aid, and Legal Awareness and Civic Engagement. You can direct your gift to any one of them, or let us allocate it where it's needed most.

What does my donation actually fund?

It funds real, specific outcomes — a tree adopted and cared for over three years, a training seat that ends in a certified job, or a family's monthly generic medicines. Nothing sits in a general pool unless you choose "Wherever It's Needed Most," and if you opt in, we'll show you exactly how your chosen cause is progressing.

Do I get an 80G tax benefit?

Donations qualify for a deduction under Section 80G. Share your PAN when you donate, and you'll get an instant email receipt right away, followed by Form 10BE — the certificate the Income Tax Department requires — by 31 May of the following year.

Is there a minimum donation amount?

For online donations, we request a minimum of Rs 100 to help cover payment processing and documentation costs. There's no upper limit, and larger or recurring gifts can be discussed directly with our team.

Is online payment secure?

Yes. Every transaction runs through a secure, PCI compliant payment gateway supporting UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, cards, and net banking. Your details are used only to process your donation and, if you opt in, to send you updates on the cause you supported.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Select Monthly under type of donation, and your chosen amount is contributed automatically each month to your selected cause, until you choose to stop. Monthly giving is what lets us commit to multi-year work like our three-year tree adoption model.
